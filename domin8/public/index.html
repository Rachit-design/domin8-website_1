who we are

Built by the community.
For the community.

We're gamers first. Domin8 started with a simple idea — give Indore's players a real home, not just a place with PCs in the corner. So we built one. We throw the LAN nights, run the tournaments, and open the doors so people can feel what gaming's actually about: the squad next to you, the clutch everyone remembers, the friends you didn't know you'd make.

Eight years in, we're not just a cafe anymore — we're a community, a culture, and a tech hub that's only getting bigger.
